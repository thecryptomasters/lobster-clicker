# Audio Credits and License Notes

All audio currently shipped with Lobster Clicker was created specifically for this project and may be distributed with the game.

- `music/lobster_lounge_loop.ogg` — original synthesized soundtrack generated by the in-repository `tools/generate_lobster_lounge.py` workflow.
- `sfx/claw_snap.wav` — original synthesized noise/percussion effect.
- `sfx/purchase.wav` — original synthesized two-note purchase effect.
- `sfx/achievement.wav` — original synthesized achievement arpeggio.
- `sfx/disco.wav` — original synthesized rare-event arpeggio.
- `sfx/molt.wav` — original synthesized Molting fanfare.

No third-party samples or recordings are embedded in these files.
