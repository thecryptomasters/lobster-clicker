# Audio Credits and License Notes

All audio currently shipped with Lobster Clicker was created specifically for this project and may be distributed with the game.

- `music/neon_coast_espionage_loop.ogg` — original 101 BPM late-1980s nocturnal espionage soundtrack generated by the in-repository `tools/generate_neon_coast_espionage.py` workflow. Its 48-bar arrangement and circularly rendered note tails create a seamless 114-second gameplay loop.
- `music/midnight_harbor_arcade_loop.ogg` — original nautical synthwave soundtrack generated by the in-repository `tools/generate_midnight_harbor_arcade.py` workflow. Every instrument and effect is synthesized locally; no samples or third-party musical material are used.
- `music/lobster_lounge_loop.ogg` — original earlier soundtrack generated by the in-repository `tools/generate_lobster_lounge.py` workflow and retained as a project archive.
- `sfx/claw_snap.wav` — original synthesized noise/percussion effect.
- `sfx/purchase.wav` — original synthesized two-note purchase effect.
- `sfx/achievement.wav` — original synthesized achievement arpeggio.
- `sfx/disco.wav` — original synthesized rare-event arpeggio.
- `sfx/molt.wav` — original synthesized Molting fanfare.

No third-party samples or recordings are embedded in these files.
